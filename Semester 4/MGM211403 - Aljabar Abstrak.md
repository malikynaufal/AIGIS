# MGM211403 — Aljabar Abstrak (Abstract Algebra)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211403
- **Course Name:** Aljabar Abstrak (Abstract Algebra)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3
- **Semester:** 4
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211102 — Aljabar Linear

## Course Description
Introduction to abstract algebraic structures and their theoretical foundations. Studies groups, rings, and fields through rigorous proofs and examples, emphasizing the underlying mathematical structures that unify various branches of mathematics.

## Key Topics

### Group Theory
- Binary operations and algebraic structures
- Subgroups, cosets, Lagrange's theorem
- Cyclic groups and generators
- Permutation groups and symmetric groups S_n
- Normal subgroups and quotient groups
- Homomorphism theorems
- Isomorphism theorems for groups
- Group actions and Burnside's lemma
- Sylow theorems (introduction)

### Ring Theory
- Definitions: rings, commutative rings, unitriangular and triangular matrices
- Subrings, ideals and quotient rings
- Ring homomorphisms and isomorphism theorems
- Polynomial rings and factor rings
- Integral domains and fields
- Unique factorization domains

### Field Theory
- Field extensions and algebraic elements
- Finite fields: GF(p), GF(2^n)
- Splitting fields and irreducible polynomials
- Algebraic closure
- Fundamental theorem of algebra (overview)

### Linear Representations of Groups
- Module theory and vector space actions
- Representation theory basics
- Characters of groups

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply abstract algebraic concepts to solve theoretical problems
- Prove fundamental theorems in group and ring theory
- Analyze algebraic structures using homomorphisms
- Work with polynomial rings and factor fields
- Understand the interconnections between linear algebra and abstract algebra
