# MGM211402 — Statistika Matematika (Mathematical Statistics)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211402
- **Course Name:** Statistika Matematika (Mathematical Statistics)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3
- **Semester:** 4
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211103 — Statistika Dasar; MGM211303 — Probabilitas

## Course Description
Rigorous treatment of statistical inference building on probability theory. Covers estimation theory, hypothesis testing, regression analysis, and analysis of variance with mathematical proofs and computational implementations.

## Key Topics

### Estimation Theory
- Point estimation: definitions, unbiasedness, consistency, efficiency
- Maximum Likelihood Estimation (MLE): derivation and properties
- Method of moments and Bayesian estimation
- Cramér-Rao lower bound
- Sufficient statistics and Fisher-Neyman factorization
- Minimum variance unbiased estimators (MVUE)
- Rao-Blackwell and Lehmann-Scheffé theorems

### Hypothesis Testing
- Statistical hypotheses: simple and composite
- Neyman-Pearson lemma and most powerful tests
- Uniformly most powerful tests
- Likelihood ratio tests
- Wald, score, and likelihood ratio tests
- p-values and significance levels
- Type I/II errors and power functions
- Multiple testing and Bonferroni correction

### Interval Estimation
- Confidence intervals: exact and asymptotic
- Pivotal quantities and construction
- Confidence intervals for means, variances, proportions
- Bootstrap confidence intervals
- Bayesian credible intervals
- Relationship between tests and confidence intervals

### Linear Models and Regression
- Simple linear regression: least squares estimation
- Properties of LSE: Gauss-Markov theorem
- Multiple linear regression: matrix formulation
- Hypothesis testing in regression
- Goodness of fit: R², adjusted R², F-test
- Residual analysis and model diagnostics
- Multicollinearity and variable selection

### Analysis of Variance (ANOVA)
- One-way and two-way ANOVA
- Fixed, random, and mixed effects models
- Multiple comparisons (Tukey, Scheffé)
- Randomized block designs
- Latin square designs

## Learning Outcomes
- Derive and apply maximum likelihood estimators
- Construct hypothesis tests with specified significance levels
- Build and interpret confidence intervals
- Fit and diagnose linear regression models
- Perform ANOVA and interpret results
- Apply statistical methods to real data using R/Python
