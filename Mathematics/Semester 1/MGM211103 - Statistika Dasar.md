# MGM211103 — Statistika Dasar (Basic Statistics)

## Course Information

- **Course Code:** MGM211103

- **Course Name:** Statistika Dasar (Basic Statistics)

- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3

- **Semester:** 1

- **Prerequisites:** SMA-level Mathematics

## Course Description
Fundamental concepts of descriptive and inferential statistics. Students learn to collect, organize, summarize, and interpret data. The course covers probability foundations, common probability distributions, and introduces basic inferential methods including estimation and hypothesis testing.

## Key Topics

### Descriptive Statistics

- Types of data: qualitative vs. quantitative, discrete vs. continuous

- Measures of central tendency: mean, median, mode, trimmed mean

- Measures of variability: range, variance, standard deviation, IQR

- Data visualization: histograms, box plots, scatter plots, stem-and-leaf

- Five-number summary and z-scores

- Covariance and correlation coefficient

### Probability Foundations

- Sample space, events, and probability axioms

- Counting principles: permutations and combinations

- Conditional probability and Bayes' Theorem

- Independence of events

- Law of Total Probability

- Random variables and probability mass/density functions

### Discrete Probability Distributions

- Bernoulli and Binomial distributions

- Poisson distribution

- Geometric and Negative Binomial distributions

- Hypergeometric distribution

- Expected value, variance, and moments

### Continuous Probability Distributions

- Uniform distribution

- Normal (Gaussian) distribution and the Standard Normal

- Exponential distribution

- Gamma and Chi-square distributions

- Central Limit Theorem and its applications

### Statistical Inference (Introduction)

- Point estimation and properties of estimators (unbiasedness, efficiency)

- Confidence intervals for means and proportions

- Hypothesis testing: null and alternative hypotheses

- Type I and Type II errors

- One-sample z-test and t-test

- P-values and decision rules

## Learning Outcomes

- Compute and interpret descriptive statistics for datasets

- Apply probability rules and counting methods to solve problems

- Identify and apply common probability distributions

- Construct confidence intervals for population parameters

- Perform basic hypothesis tests and interpret results

- Use statistical reasoning in data-driven decision making
