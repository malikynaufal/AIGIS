# MGM211107 — Pendidikan Pancasila (Pancasila Education)

## Course Information

- **Course Code:** MGM211107

- **Course Name:** Pendidikan Pancasila (Pancasila Education)

- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 2

- **Semester:** 1

- **Prerequisites:** None

## Course Description
Study of Pancasila as the philosophical foundation of the Indonesian state. This course examines the historical development, philosophical content, and practical implementation of the five principles of Pancasila in national life, with emphasis on their relevance in the digital age and technology-driven society.

## Key Topics

### Historical Foundations

- Pre-colonial philosophical roots of Pancasila

- The Youth Pledge (Sumpah Pemuda) of 1928

- Formulation at BPUPKI (May–June 1945)

- Jakarta Charter and its implications

- Constitutional debates and Pancasila's emergence

- Sukarno's August 17, 1945 speech and its philosophical content

### The Five Principles (Sila)

- **Sila 1 — Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa**: Monotheism and religious pluralism
  - Freedom of religion and its boundaries
  - Harmony among religious communities

- **Sila 2 — Kemanusiaan yang Adil dan Beradab**: Human dignity and justice
  - Human rights in the Indonesian context
  - Social justice and equality

- **Sila 3 — Persatuan Indonesia**: National unity
  - Unity in diversity (Bhinneka Tunggal Ika)
  - National integration and regional autonomy

- **Sila 4 — Kerakyatan yang Dipimpin oleh Hikmat Kebijaksanaan dalam Permusyawaratan/Perwakilan**: Democracy
  - Deliberation (musyawarah) and consensus (mufakat)
  - Representative democracy in Indonesia

- **Sila 5 — Keadilan Sosial bagi Seluruh Rakyat Indonesia**: Social justice
  - Economic equity and welfare
  - Distribution of national prosperity

### Pancasila and Technology Ethics

- Digital ethics from a Pancasila perspective

- Responsible use of technology and social media

- Intellectual property and open-source philosophy

- AI ethics and Pancasila values

- National data sovereignty

### Contemporary Issues

- Pancasila in the reformasi era

- Countering radicalism and extremism

- Pancasila in education and civic engagement

- Global citizenship and national identity

- Role of Pancasila in ASEAN and international relations

## Learning Outcomes

- Explain the historical development and philosophical content of Pancasila

- Analyze contemporary issues through the lens of Pancasila principles

- Apply Pancasila values in professional and ethical decision-making

- Evaluate the relevance of Pancasila in the digital age

- Demonstrate understanding of Indonesia's constitutional and governance framework
