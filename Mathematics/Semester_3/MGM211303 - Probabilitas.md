# MGM211303 — Probabilitas (Probability Theory)

## Course Information

- **Course Code:** MGM211303

- **Course Name:** Probabilitas (Probability Theory)

- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3

- **Semester:** 3

- **Prerequisites:** MGM211101 — Kalkulus I; MGM211103 — Statistika Dasar

## Course Description
Rigorous treatment of mathematical probability covering probability spaces, random variables, expectation, limit theorems, and stochastic processes. This course builds the theoretical foundation for statistical inference, machine learning, and probabilistic modeling.

## Key Topics

### Probability Spaces

- Sigma-algebras and measure-theoretic foundations

- Sample spaces, events, and probability measures

- Self-consistency of probability operations

- Continuity of probability measures

- Monty Hall and paradoxes of probability

- Cartesian product spaces

### Random Variables

- Random variables as functions on probability spaces

- Probability distribution functions

- Cumulative distribution functions

- Probability mass functions (discrete)

- Probability density functions (continuous)

- Transformations of random variables

### Expectation and Moments

- Mathematical expectation: definition and properties

- Moment generating functions

- Characteristic functions (Fourier transforms)

- Means, variances, and moments of order distributions

- Jensen's inequality

- Chebyshev, Markov, and Chernoff inequalities

- Conditional expectation

### Multivariate Probability

- Joint distributions: discrete, continuous, mixed

- Marginal and conditional distributions

- Covariance and correlation

- Conditional expectation applications

- Multivariate normal distribution

- Law of iterated expectations (Tower property)

- Cramér-Wold theorem and characterization of distributions

### Sequences of Random Variables

- Convergence in probability (weak law of large numbers)

- Almost sure convergence (strong law of large numbers)

- Convergence in distribution (weak convergence)

- Central Limit Theorem (classical and Lindeberg-Feller)

- Delta method

- Slutsky's theorem

### Basic Stochastic Processes

- Bernoulli and random walk processes

- Poisson process definition and properties

- Markov chains: transition matrices, stationary distribution

- Birth-death processes

- Martingale concepts

## Learning Outcomes

- Construct probability spaces and compute probabilities

- Manipulate probability distributions of random variables

- Compute expectations, variances, and moments

- Apply limit theorems to analyze convergence

- Derive distributions of functions of random variables

- Model random phenomena using stochastic processes
