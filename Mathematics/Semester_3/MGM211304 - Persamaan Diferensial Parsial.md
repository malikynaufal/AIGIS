# MGM211304 — Persamaan Diferensial Parsial (Partial Differential Equations)

## Course Information

- **Course Code:** MGM211304

- **Course Name:** Persamaan Diferensial Parsial (Partial Differential Equations)

- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3

- **Semester:** 3

- **Prerequisites:** MGM211201 — Kalkulus II; MGM211202 — Persamaan Diferensial

## Course Description
Introduction to partial differential equations (PDEs) modeling physical phenomena. Covers classification, solution methods including separation of variables and Fourier series, and numerical approaches for boundary value problems.

## Key Topics

### Classification of PDEs

- Linear, semilinear, quasilinear, fully nonlinear

- Order of PDEs

- Classical classification: elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic

- Characteristic curves and surfaces

- Well-posedness and ill-posedness of problems

### First-Order PDEs

- Method of characteristics for first-order equations

- Lagrange's method: dx/P = dy/Q = dz/R

- Cauchy problems and initial value problems

- Quasi-linear first-order equations

- Conservation laws and shock waves (introduction)

### Second-Order Linear PDEs

- Canonical forms: reduction to standard types

- Wave equation (hyperbolic): d'Alembert's solution

- Heat equation (parabolic): maximum principle

- Laplace/Poisson equation (elliptic): mean value property

- Superposition principle for linear equations

### Boundary Value Problems

- Dirichlet, Neumann, and Robin boundary conditions

- Uniqueness theorems (energy methods)

- Separation of variables method

- Eigenvalue problems for Sturm-Liouville operators

- Orthogonality of eigenfunctions

### Fourier Series and Transforms

- Fourier series on bounded intervals

- Fourier series: convergence theorems

- Full-range and half-range expansions

- Fourier transform for unbounded domains

- Applications to solving PDEs on infinite/semi-infinite domains

### Advanced Solution Methods

- Green's functions for PDEs

- Method of images

- Convolution solutions

- Numerical methods overview: finite differences, finite elements

- Variational methods introduction

## Learning Outcomes

- Classify PDEs and identify appropriate solution methods

- Apply separation of variables to solve boundary value problems

- Use Fourier series and transforms to solve PDEs

- Analyze well-posedness of PDE problems

- Construct Green's functions for simple domains

- Implement basic finite difference schemes for PDEs
