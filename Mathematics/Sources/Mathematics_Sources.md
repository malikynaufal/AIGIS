# Mathematics Sources

## Core Textbooks

### Calculus & Analysis
- Stewart, J. (2015). *Calculus: Early Transcendentals*. Cengage.
- Larson, R. & Edwards, B. (2018). *Calculus*. Cengage.
- Thomas, G.B. et al. (2014). *Thomas' Calculus*. Pearson.
- Spivak, M. (1994). *Calculus*. Publish or Perish.

### Linear Algebra
- Strang, G. (2016). *Introduction to Linear Algebra*. Wellesley-Cambridge.
- Lay, D.C. et al. (2015). *Linear Algebra and Its Applications*. Pearson.
- Anton, H. et al. (2014). *Elementary Linear Algebra*. Wiley.

### Probability & Statistics
- Devore, J.L. (2017). *Probability and Statistics for Engineering and the Sciences*. Cengage.
- Ross, S.M. (2014). *A First Course in Probability*. Pearson.
- Walpole, R.E. et al. (2016). *Probability & Statistics for Engineers & Scientists*. Pearson.

### Numerical Methods
- Burden, R.L. & Faires, J.D. (2010). *Numerical Analysis*. Brooks/Cole.
- Chapra, S.C. & Canale, R.P. (2014). *Numerical Methods for Engineers*. McGraw-Hill.
- Heath, M.T. (2018). *Scientific Computing*. McGraw-Hill.
