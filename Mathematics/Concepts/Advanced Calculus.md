# Advanced Calculus

## Overview

Advanced calculus extends single-variable concepts to multiple variables, differential forms, and manifolds. In geodesy, advanced calculus provides the mathematical framework for differential geometry on the ellipsoid, potential theory, and the analysis of gravity fields.

## Core Topics

### Vector Calculus

- Gradient, divergence, curl

- Line and surface integrals

- Green's, Stokes', and Divergence theorems

### Differential Forms

- Exterior derivative

- Manifolds and integration on manifolds

- Stokes' theorem in generalized form

### Applications to Geodesy

- Surface integrals on the ellipsoid

- Potential theory in spherical coordinates

- Geodesic curvature and the Gauss-Bonnet theorem

## Key Formulas
$$\nabla \times \mathbf{F} = \begin{vmatrix} \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\ \partial/\partial x & \partial/\partial y & \partial/\partial z \\ F_x & F_y & F_z \end{vmatrix}$$

$$\iint_S \nabla \times \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \oint_{\partial S} \mathbf{F} \cdot d\mathbf{r}$$
