# Semester 6 — Riset Operasi (Expanded)

**Course**: MGM211601 — Riset Operasi 
**Credits**: 3 SKS 
**Prerequisites**: [[Optimisasi Expanded]], [[Aljabar Linear Lanjut Expanded]]

---

## Course Overview

Operations research applies mathematical optimization to complex decision problems. Topics include linear programming, network flows, integer programming, and dynamic programming.

---

## Syllabus

### Unit 1: Linear Programming

- **Formulation**: Decision variables, objective function, constraints

- **Graphical method**: For 2-variable problems

- **Simplex method**: Tableau form, pivoting rules

- **Duality**: Economic interpretation, shadow prices

- **Sensitivity analysis**: Post-optimal analysis

### Unit 2: Network Optimization

- **Shortest path**: Dijkstra, Bellman-Ford

- **Minimum spanning tree**: Kruskal, Prim

- **Maximum flow**: Ford-Fulkerson, Edmonds-Karp

- **Transportation problem**: Specialized simplex method

- **Assignment problem**: Hungarian algorithm

### Unit 3: Integer Programming

- **Formulations**: Binary variables, fixed costs

- **Branch and bound**: Systematic enumeration

- **Cutting planes**: Gomory cuts

- **Mixed-integer programming**: MILP solvers

### Unit 4: Dynamic Programming

- **Principle of optimality**: Optimal substructure

- **Recursive formulation**: Bellman equation

- **Applications**: Resource allocation, shortest path

- **Memoization**: Avoiding recomputation

### Unit 5: Decision Analysis

- **Decision trees**: Sequential decisions

- **Expected value**: Risk-neutral criterion

- **Utility theory**: Risk attitudes

- **Sensitivity analysis**: Value of information

---

## Geodesy Applications

- **Survey planning**: Optimal station placement

- **Network design**: Minimum cost observation networks

- **Resource allocation**: Distributing field teams

- **Scheduling**: Survey campaign planning
