# MGM211404 — Analisis Kompleks (Complex Analysis)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211404
- **Course Name:** Analisis Kompleks (Complex Analysis)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3
- **Semester:** 4
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211201 — Kalkulus II

## Course Description
Study of functions of a complex variable, analytic functions, and their applications. This course covers the fundamental theorems of complex analysis including Cauchy's theorems, residue calculus, and conformal mappings with applications to physics and engineering.

## Key Topics

### Complex Numbers and Functions
- Algebra and geometry of complex numbers
- Polar and exponential forms
- Roots of unity and nth roots of complex numbers
- Complex functions: domain, range, and continuity
- Analytic (holomorphic) functions
- Cauchy-Riemann equations and conformality
- Harmonic functions and their relationship to analytic functions

### Complex Differentiation
- Complex derivatives and limits
- Power series and their convergence
- Elementary functions: exp, log, trigonometric, hyperbolic
- Branch cuts and multi-valued functions
- Analytic continuation

### Integration
- Contour integrals of complex functions
- Cauchy's Integral Theorem
- Cauchy's Integral Formula
- Cauchy's Inequality and Liouville's Theorem
- Maximum modulus principle and minimum principle
- Fundamental theorem of algebra (proof)

### Residue Theory
- Singularities: removable, poles, essential
- Laurent series
- Residue definition and computation
- Residue Theorem
- Real integrals using residues
- Evaluation of definite integrals
- Applications to summation of series
- Jordan's lemma and improper integrals

### Conformal Mapping
- Conformal mappings and Möbius transformations
- Preserved properties: angles, circles
- Key transformations: translation, rotation, inversion
- Riemann mapping theorem (statement)
- Schwarz-Christoffel transformation
- Applications to electrostatics, fluid flow, and heat

### Additional Topics
- Argument principle
- Rouche's theorem
- Maximum modulus principle revisited
- Picard's little and great theorems
- Introduction to Riemann surfaces

## Learning Outcomes
- Analyze functions for analyticity using Cauchy-Riemann equations
- Compute contour integrals using Cauchy's theorems
- Apply residue calculus to evaluate real and complex integrals
- Use conformal mappings to transform and solve boundary value problems
- Work with multi-valued functions and branch cuts
- Connect complex analysis methods to real-world applications
