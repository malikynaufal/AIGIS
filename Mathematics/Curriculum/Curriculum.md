# 📘 Mathematics Curriculum — 8 Semester Roadmap

## UGM Teknik Geodesi — Studi Komputer (S.Kom)

### Overview
This curriculum guides your mathematics education across 8 semesters, building from foundational calculus and linear algebra through advanced topics in optimization, machine learning, and data science for geospatial applications.

## Learning Pathway for Geodesy Students

1. **Semester 1-2**: Build calculus and linear algebra foundation
2. **Semester 3-4**: Develop computational and statistical methods
3. **Semester 5-6**: Apply to geospatial and data problems
4. **Semester 7-8**: Specialize and complete capstone project

---

## Key Competencies

- ✅ Mathematical modeling for geodetic problems
- ✅ Statistical analysis for spatial data
- ✅ Numerical algorithms for computation
- ✅ Machine learning for pattern recognition
- ✅ Software development for geospatial tools
