# MGM211302 — Analisis Real (Real Analysis)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211302
- **Course Name:** Analisis Real (Real Analysis)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3
- **Semester:** 3
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211101, MGM211102

## Course Description
Rigorous study of the real number system and foundation of analysis. This course develops mathematical thinking with precise definitions and proofs, covering sequences, series, continuity, differentiability, and foundational topology of the real numbers.

## Key Topics

### Foundational Concepts
- The real number system and its properties
- Completeness axiom and its consequences
- Rational vs. real numbers: gaps in Q, irrational numbers
- Countable and uncountable sets
- Supremum and infimum of sets
- Order and absolute value properties

### Sequences
- Convergence and divergence of sequences
- Monotone sequences and boundedness
- Bolzano-Weierstrass theorem: every bounded sequence has convergent subsequence
- Cauchy sequences and completeness
- Limit theorems for sequences
- Squeeze theorem for sequences

### Continuity
- Definition and properties of continuous functions
- Intermediate Value Theorem
- Uniform continuity
- Continuity in metric spaces
- Sequential criterion for continuity

### Differentiation
- Derivatives: definition and existence conditions
- Rolle's Theorem and Mean Value Theorems
- Monotonicity and the relationship between derivatives and function behavior
- L'Hôpital's rule for limits
- Taylor's theorem with Lagrange remainder
- Approximation of functions

### Integration Theory
- Riemann integral: definition and properties
- Monotone convergence theorem
- Continuity implies integrability, Darboux approach
- Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (Part 1)
- Improper integrals

### Series
- Infinite series of real numbers
- Convergence tests: comparison, ratio, root, integral tests
- Alternating series and Leibniz criterion
- Absolute vs. conditional convergence
- Power series: radius and interval of convergence
- Term-by-term operations on power series
- Applications to function approximation

## Learning Outcomes
- Work with formal proofs involving real numbers and sequences
- Determine convergence of sequences and series rigorously
- Apply epsilon-delta definitions to analyze continuity and differentiability
- Use Taylor polynomials for function approximation
- Prove and apply the main theorems of real analysis
- Develop mathematical writing and rigorous reasoning skills
