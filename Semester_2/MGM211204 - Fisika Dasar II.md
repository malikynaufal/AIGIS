# MGM211204 — Fisika Dasar II (Basic Physics II)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211204
- **Course Name:** Fisika Dasar II (Basic Physics II)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 4
- **Semester:** 2
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211104 — Fisika Dasar I

## Course Description
Continuation of Basic Physics I covering thermodynamics, electrostatics, electric circuits, magnetism, and electromagnetic waves. Provides physical foundation for understanding computing hardware, signal processing, and electromagnetic modeling in computer science.

## Key Topics

### Thermodynamics
- Temperature and thermal equilibrium
- Heat transfer: conduction, convection, radiation
- First law of thermodynamics and internal energy
- Ideal gas law and kinetic theory
- Thermodynamic processes: isothermal, isobaric, isochoric, adiabatic
- Heat engines and Carnot cycle
- Second law of thermodynamics and entropy

### Electrostatics
- Electric charge and Coulomb's Law
- Electric field: calculation and visualization
- Gauss's Law and its applications
- Electric potential and potential energy
- Capacitors and dielectrics
- Capacitance: parallel plate, spherical, cylindrical
- Energy stored in electric fields

### Electric Circuits
- Current, voltage, and resistance
- Ohm's Law and resistivity
- Kirchhoff's laws (junction and loop rules)
- Series and parallel circuits
- RC circuits: charging and discharging
- AC circuits: impedance, phase, resonance
- Power in AC and DC circuits

### Magnetism
- Magnetic fields and forces on charged particles
- Magnetic force on current-carrying wires
- Biot-Savart Law and Ampère's Law
- Faraday's Law of electromagnetic induction
- Lenz's Law
- Inductors and inductance
- Transformers and mutual inductance

### Electromagnetic Waves
- Maxwell's equations (conceptual overview)
- Electromagnetic wave equation
- Properties of EM waves: wavelength, frequency, speed
- The electromagnetic spectrum
- Polarization and interference
- Applications: wireless communication, fiber optics

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply thermodynamic laws to analyze heat and energy systems
- Calculate electric fields and potentials using Gauss's Law
- Analyze DC and AC circuits with Kirchhoff's methods
- Predict magnetic forces and induced EMF in physical systems
- Understand Maxwell's unification of electricity and magnetism
- Connect electromagnetic principles to computing and communication technologies
