# MGM211201 — Kalkulus II (Calculus II)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211201
- **Course Name:** Kalkulus II (Calculus II)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 4
- **Semester:** 2
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211101 — Kalkulus I

## Course Description
Continuation of Calculus I focusing on advanced integration techniques, applications of integrals, infinite sequences and series, and parametric/polar coordinates. Students develop sophisticated techniques for evaluating integrals and learn to analyze convergence of infinite series.

## Key Topics

### Techniques of Integration
- Integration by parts: formula and strategy
- Trigonometric integrals and substitutions
- Partial fraction decomposition
- Integration of rational functions
- Improper integrals: Type I (unbounded intervals) and Type II (discontinuities)
- Comparison test for improper integrals
- Numerical integration: Trapezoidal Rule, Simpson's Rule

### Applications of Integration
- Area between curves
- Volumes of revolution (disk, washer, shell methods)
- Arc length and surface area of revolution
- Work done by variable forces
- Hydrostatic pressure and force
- Centers of mass and moments (centroid, center of pressure)
- Probability density functions and expected values

### Infinite Sequences
- Definition and convergence of sequences
- Monotone Convergence Theorem
- Limit laws for sequences
- Squeeze Theorem applications
- Bounded sequences and Bolzano-Weierstrass Theorem

### Infinite Series
- Geometric series and p-series
- Convergence tests: divergence, integral, comparison, limit comparison
- Ratio test, root test, and Raabe's test
- Alternating series test and Leibniz's theorem
- Absolute vs. conditional convergence
- Power series: radius and interval of convergence
- Taylor and Maclaurin series
- Taylor's Theorem with remainder
- Applications of Taylor series (approximations, evaluating integrals)

### Parametric and Polar Coordinates
- Parametric curves: derivatives and arc length
- Polar coordinates: conversion formulas
- Area and arc length in polar coordinates
- Conic sections in polar form

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply advanced integration techniques to evaluate complex integrals
- Compute volumes, arc lengths, and areas using definite integrals
- Determine convergence or divergence of sequences and series
- Construct Taylor and Maclaurin series representations
- Work effectively in parametric and polar coordinate systems
- Apply improper integrals to solve applied probability problems
