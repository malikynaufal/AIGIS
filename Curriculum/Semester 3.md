# 📙 Semester 3: Reference Frames & Geospatial Data

## Overview
Exploring the digital representation of the Earth and the rigorous reference frames required for global positioning.

## ✍️ Core Courses

### 1. Sistem Referensi Geodesi (Geodetic Reference Systems)
- **Topics**: ITRS/ITRF, WGS84, GRS80, and regional datums (DGN95 in Indonesia).
- **Key Concept**: The movement of tectonic plates and its impact on static datums.

### 2. Sistem Informasi Geografis (Geographic Information Systems)
- **Topics**: Vector vs. Raster data models, topology, SQL for spatial databases, and web mapping.
- **Software**: QGIS, ArcGIS, PostGIS.

### 3. Geometri Diferensial (Differential Geometry)
- **Topics**: Curves and surfaces in 3D space, Gaussian curvature, and geodesics on the ellipsoid.
- **Formula**: $K = k_1 k_2$ (Product of principal curvatures).

### 4. Oseanografi Fisis (Physical Oceanography)
- **Geodetic Link**: Tidal analysis, Mean Sea Level (MSL), and the definition of the Vertical Datum.
