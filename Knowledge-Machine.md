# 🧠 AIGIS Knowledge Learning Genius

> Your personal research companion that digs up the best open‑access resources, builds structured notes, and keeps everything synced to NotebookLM — so you learn efficiently, from fundamentals to advanced.

*I don't just store notes. I actively research, curate, and connect — just like a genius tutor should.*

## How it works

1. **I research for you** — daily I scan open‑access repositories (MIT OCW, LibreTexts, arXiv, IAG, FIG, university lecture notes) for the best textbooks, papers, and courses relevant to Geodesy.
2. **I structure the knowledge** — every resource gets curated, verified for legality, and filed into the right subject folder with a clear *why it's useful for geodesy* note.
3. **I build progressive notes** — your vault grows from fundamentals (what is a coordinate system?) to advanced (satellite‑based reference frames), with each concept linked to the next.
4. **I sync everything to NotebookLM** — real‑time through your Google Drive (`H:` & `G:`), so your notebooks always have fresh material.
5. **I talk to you on Telegram** — ask me anything, request study packs, or tell me to research a new topic.

## What each folder is for

| Folder | Content |
|--------|---------|
| `Mathematics/Resources/` | Open‑access textbooks, MIT OCW, key papers with Geodesy context |
| `Physics/Resources/` | Open‑access Physics resources tailored to Geodesy needs |
| `Geodesy/Resources/` | Geodesy‑specific papers, IAG/FIG publications, lecture notes |
| `_Inbox/` | Drop any content here with a `target:` line — AIGIS files it |
| `_Study Packs/` | Curated, subject‑spanning study packs generated on request |

## How to use me

- **Explore the Resource tables** — each entry links to a legal, freely‑available source I've already vetted.

- **Ask me on Telegram** *"research [topic]"*, *"study pack on [concept]"*, *"quiz me on [topic]"*, or *"explain [concept] like a genius tutor"*.

- **Open NotebookLM** and see new content appear as I build it — no manual uploads needed.

- **Every new source** I find is checked for legality and relevance before I include it.

> *I'm not a static file dump. I'm a learning partner that grows with you — research, structure, teach, repeat.*
