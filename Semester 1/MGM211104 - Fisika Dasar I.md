# MGM211104 — Fisika Dasar I (Basic Physics I)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211104
- **Course Name:** Fisika Dasar I (Basic Physics I)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 4
- **Semester:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** SMA-level Mathematics and Physics

## Course Description
Introduction to classical mechanics covering kinematics, Newton's laws of motion, energy and momentum conservation, rotational dynamics, and oscillatory motion. This course provides the physical intuition and mathematical framework essential for computational modeling of physical systems.

## Key Topics

### Kinematics
- Displacement, velocity, and acceleration in one and two dimensions
- Projectile motion and relative motion
- Circular motion and centripetal acceleration
- Vector analysis in kinematics
- Motion graphs and their interpretation

### Newton's Laws of Motion
- Newton's three laws and their applications
- Free-body diagrams and force analysis
- Friction: static and kinetic
- Normal force, tension, and spring forces
- Non-inertial reference frames

### Work, Energy, and Power
- Work done by constant and variable forces
- Kinetic energy and the Work-Energy Theorem
- Potential energy (gravitational, elastic)
- Conservative forces and potential energy functions
- Conservation of mechanical energy
- Power and efficiency

### Momentum and Collisions
- Linear momentum and impulse
- Conservation of momentum
- Elastic, inelastic, and perfectly inelastic collisions
- Center of mass motion
- Variable-mass systems (rockets)

### Rotational Motion
- Angular position, velocity, and acceleration
- Torque and moment of inertia
- Rotational kinetic energy and work
- Angular momentum and conservation
- Rolling motion without slipping

### Oscillations and Waves
- Simple harmonic motion (mass-spring, pendulum)
- Damped harmonic motion
- Forced oscillations and resonance
- Introduction to wave propagation

## Learning Outcomes
- Apply kinematic equations to predict motion in 1D and 2D
- Construct free-body diagrams and apply Newton's laws
- Use energy conservation to solve mechanics problems
- Analyze momentum conservation in collision scenarios
- Compute rotational quantities and apply conservation laws
- Model oscillatory systems mathematically
