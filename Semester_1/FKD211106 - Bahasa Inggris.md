# FKD211106 — Bahasa Inggris untuk Fisika

## Course Overview

English for Physics — developing the reading, writing, and communication skills necessary for engaging with the international scientific community. Physics is a global language, and mastery of English terminology, academic writing conventions, and presentation skills is essential for any physicist or geodesist.

**Contact Hours:** 2 SKS (2 hours per week)
**Prerequisites:** None
**Co-requisites:** None

---

## 📋 Topics & Outline

### Unit 1: Scientific English Foundations (Weeks 1–4)
- **Physics vocabulary** in English: mechanics, energy, force, mass, acceleration
- Reading scientific texts: identifying structure (Abstract, Introduction, Methods, Results, Discussion)
- Active vs. passive voice in scientific writing
- Quantitative expressions: units, magnitudes, comparisons
- **Example physics terms:**
  ```
  kinematics → kinematika        dynamics → dinamika
  electromagnetism → elektromagnetika    thermodynamics → termodinamika
  wavelength → panjang gelombang        frequency → frekuensi
  gravitational potential → potensi gravitasi   geoid → geoid
  ```

### Unit 2: Reading Scientific Papers (Weeks 5–8)
- Structure of a research paper: IMRAD format
- Skimming and scanning techniques for efficiency
- Understanding abstracts and formulating questions
- Vocabulary building: Greek/Latin roots in physics
  ```
  -philic = loving, -phobic = fearing
  -metry = measurement, -scope = viewing
  geo- = Earth, astro- = star, -sphere = ball
  ```
- Critical reading: identifying assumptions, evaluating evidence
- Practice reading papers from *Journal of Geophysical Research*

### Unit 3: Scientific Writing (Weeks 9–12)
- **Lab report structure:** title, abstract, introduction, methods, results, discussion
- Writing clear and concise scientific sentences
- Citing references: APA, IEEE styles
- Describing data and trends in words and equations
- Formal vs. informal scientific register
- Common grammar pitfalls for Indonesian speakers
- Peer review and revision process

### Unit 4: Scientific Presentation (Weeks 13–16)
- Oral presentation skills: structure, pacing, visual aids
- **PowerPoint/poster design** for scientific content
- Handling Q&A sessions
- Video conference communication (increasingly important post-2020)
- Writing an email to a professor or journal editor
- Career English: CVs, cover letters, recommendation letters
- Final presentation: explain a physics concept in English

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:
1. Read and comprehend English-language physics textbooks and papers
2. Write clear, structured scientific reports in English
3. Use physics terminology accurately in English
4. Deliver oral presentations on physics topics in English
5. Communicate professionally via email and in academic contexts
6. Navigate English-language online resources (MIT OCW, arXiv, etc.)
