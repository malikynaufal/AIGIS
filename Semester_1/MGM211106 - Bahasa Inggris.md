# MGM211106 — Bahasa Inggris (English for Academic Purposes)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211106
- **Course Name:** Bahasa Inggris (English for Academic Purposes)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 2
- **Semester:** 1
- **Prerequisites:** Basic English proficiency

## Course Description
English language course tailored for mathematics and computer science students. Focuses on academic reading, technical writing, and oral presentation skills necessary for understanding international textbooks, research papers, and participating in global academic discourse.

## Key Topics

### Academic Reading Skills
- Skimming, scanning, and close reading strategies
- Understanding technical vocabulary in context
- Reading mathematical and scientific texts
- Analyzing research paper structure (IMRaD format)
- Critical reading: evaluating arguments and evidence
- Summarizing and synthesizing information from multiple sources

### Technical Writing
- Formal academic writing style and conventions
- Writing definitions, classifications, and descriptions
- Describing mathematical processes and algorithms
- Structuring technical reports and lab reports
- Citation styles: IEEE, APA, and ACM formats
- Avoiding plagiarism and academic integrity

### Oral Communication
- Presenting mathematical concepts clearly
- Structuring effective technical presentations
- Using visual aids (slides, diagrams, equations)
- Handling Q&A sessions confidently
- Participating in academic discussions and seminars
- Pronunciation of mathematical symbols and terminology

### Discipline-Specific Vocabulary
- Mathematical terminology (sets, functions, limits, derivatives, integrals)
- Linear algebra vocabulary (vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, subspaces)
- Statistics and probability terms (random variables, distributions, hypothesis)
- Computer science terms (algorithms, data structures, complexity)
- Logical connectives and proof language

### Grammar for Academic English
- Passive voice in scientific writing
- Conditional sentences for hypotheses
- Modal verbs for hedging and certainty
- Relative clauses for definitions
- Parallel structure in lists and comparisons

## Learning Outcomes
- Read and comprehend technical mathematics and CS texts
- Write clear, structured academic reports and papers
- Deliver effective technical presentations in English
- Use discipline-specific vocabulary accurately
- Apply appropriate citation and referencing conventions
- Communicate mathematical reasoning in written and spoken English
