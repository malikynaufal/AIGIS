# MGM211601 — Riset Operasi (Operations Research)

## Course Information
- **Course Code:** MGM211601
- **Course Name:** Riset Operasi (Operations Research)
- **Credit Hours (SKS):** 3
- **Semester:** 6
- **Prerequisites:** MGM211501 — Metode Numerik; MGM211503 — Optimisasi

## Course Description
Mathematical modeling and optimization of decision-making problems in complex systems. Covers decision theory, inventory control, queuing theory, and simulation with applications to operations management and logistics.

## Key Topics

### Decision Theory
- Decision making under certainty
- Decision matrices and payoff tables
- Maximin, maximax, and minimax regret criteria
- Decision making under risk (expected monetary value)
- Expected value of perfect and imperfect information
- Utility theory and risk aversion
- Multi-criteria decision analysis (MCDM)

### Inventory Models
- EOQ (Economic Order Quantity) and variants
- Inventory with probabilistic demand
- Safety stock and reorder point
- Multi-item inventory models
- Supply chain coordination

### Queuing Theory
- Markovian queuing models (M/M/1, M/M/c, M/M/∞)
- Little's Law and its applications
- System performance measures (utilization, wait time)
- Priority queuing systems
- Non-Markovian models (introduction)

### Network Optimization
- Transportation problem and algorithms
- Assignment problem (Hungarian method)
- Shortest path and minimum spanning tree
- Maximum flow and minimum cut
- Network design and reliability

### Project Management
- Project scheduling: CPM and PERT
- Critical path analysis
- Resource allocation and leveling
- Time-cost trade-off (crashing)
- Gantt charts and scheduling tools

### Decision Support Systems
- Linear and goal programming for decision support
- Decision trees and sequential analysis
- Sensitivity analysis in decision models
- Data-driven decision making

## Learning Outcomes
- Formulate decision problems using OR models
- Apply queuing theory to analyze service systems
- Solve inventory management problems using EOQ models
- Optimize project schedules using CPM/PERT methods
- Model and solve network optimization problems
- Build decision support models for operational problems
