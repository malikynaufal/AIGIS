# FKD211405 — Laboratorium Fisika Dasar II

## Course Overview

Experimental electromagnetism — this laboratory course directly complements the theoretical content of Fisika Dasar II and Elektromagnetik. Students build and measure circuits, construct electromagnetic devices, observe wave phenomena, and develop deeper understanding of fields, potentials, and signals through hands-on experimentation.

**Contact Hours:** 2 SKS (4 hours lab per week, biweekly)
**Prerequisites:** Fisika Dasar II, Kalkulus II
**Co-requisites:** Elektromagnetik

---

## 📋 Topics & Outline

### Experiment 1: DC Circuit Analysis (Weeks 1–2)

- Digital multimeter operation: measuring V, I, R

- Building series and parallel circuits on breadboards

- **Verifying Kirchhoff's laws:**
 - Voltage divider rule: V_R1 = V_s · R₁/(R₁+R₂)
 - Current divider principle

- **Thevenin and Norton equivalent circuits**

- Measuring internal resistance of a voltage source

### Experiment 2: RC Circuits and Time Constants (Weeks 3–4)

- **Capacitor charging/discharging** through a resistor
 - V_c(t) = V₀(1 - e^{-t/RC}) for charging
 - V_c(t) = V₀e^{-t/RC} for discharging

- Measuring time constant τ = RC from exponential fits

- Comparing theoretical vs. measured τ

- Effect of capacitance on circuit response time

### Experiment 3: Magnetic Fields and Induction (Weeks 5–6)

- Mapping magnetic fields of bar magnets using Hall probe

- **Magnetic field of a solenoid:** B = μ₀nI

- **Faraday's law:** measuring induced emf for varying flux

- Moving magnet through coil and observing induced current

- Transformer principle: V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p

### Experiment 4: Oscilloscopes and AC Circuits (Weeks 7–8)

- **Oscilloscope operation:** triggering, timebase, voltage scaling

- Measuring amplitude, frequency, and phase of AC signals

- Lissajous figures: measuring phase difference

- **Function generator** and signal characteristics

- RC filter: low-pass and high-pass behavior

### Experiment 5: RLC Circuits and Resonance (Weeks 9–10)

- **Series RLC:** measuring resonant frequency f₀ = 1/(2π√(LC))

- Frequency response: measuring V_R(f) across the resistor

- **Quality factor Q:** Q = f₀/Δf (bandwidth)

- Impedance measurement as function of frequency

- Phase shift at resonance (φ = 0)

### Experiment 6: Electromagnetic Induction (Weeks 11–12)

- **Build a simple generator:** coil rotation in magnetic field

- Measuring output voltage vs. rotation speed

- **Transformer characteristics:** step-up, step-down

- Mutual inductance measurement

### Experiment 7: Optics Experiments (Weeks 13–14)

- **Snell's law verification** using laser and prism

- **Thin lens:** measuring focal length by lens equation

- **Diffraction grating:** measuring laser wavelength
 - λ = d sin θ / m

### Experiment 8: Electromagnetic Waves (Weeks 15–16)

- **Antenna radiation:** simple dipole antenna pattern measurement

- Wave polarization detection

- Signal attenuation with distance

- Absorption by materials (EM shielding)

## 🔬 Key Experimental Techniques

```
Voltage divider: V_out = V_in · R₂/(R₁+R₂)
RC charging: V(t) = V₀(1 - e^{-t/RC})
Resonance: f₀ = 1/(2π√(LC))
Solenoid field: B = μ₀nI
Thin lens: 1/f = 1/s₀ + 1/s₁
Diffraction: d sin θ = mλ
```

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:
1. Use oscilloscopes, signal generators, and multimeters with proficiency
2. Build and troubleshoot circuits from schematic diagrams
3. Measure and analyze frequency response and resonance
4. Verify Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction experimentally
5. Observe optical phenomena and measure key constants (e.g., laser wavelength)
6. Write comprehensive lab reports on EM experiments
