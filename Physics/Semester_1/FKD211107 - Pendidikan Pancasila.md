# FKD211107 — Pendidikan Pancasila

## Course Overview

Study of Pancasila as the philosophical foundation of the Indonesian state — the five principles that guide national identity, governance, and civic responsibility. For physics students, this course provides context for the ethical and social dimensions of scientific work within the Indonesian national framework.

**Contact Hours:** 2 SKS (2 hours per week)
**Prerequisites:** None
**Co-requisites:** None

---

## 📋 Topics & Outline

### Unit 1: Historical Foundations (Weeks 1–4)

- Pre-independence Indonesia: colonial era and independence movement

- **Youth Pledge (Sumpah Pemuda)** 1928: one nation, one language, one homeland

- The road to independence: 1945 Proclamation

- **BPUPKI sessions:** debating the philosophical basis of the state

- Role of Soekarno, Hatta, and other founding figures

- The formulation of Pancasila: historical context and compromise

### Unit 2: The Five Principles (Weeks 5–10)

- **Silas (Pillars):**
 1. **Ketuhanan Yang Maha Esa** — Belief in the One and Only God
 2. **Kemanusiaan yang Adil dan Beradab** — Just and Civilized Humanity
 3. **Persatuan Indonesia** — Unity of Indonesia
 4. **Kerakyatan yang Dipimpin oleh Hikmat Kebijaksanaan dalam Permusyawaratan/Perwakilan** — Democracy guided by wisdom in deliberation/representation
 5. **Keadilan Sosial bagi Seluruh Rakyat Indonesia** — Social Justice for All Indonesians

- Each Sila in depth: philosophical origins, interpretations, applications

- Pancasila vs. other national ideologies (liberalism, communism, fascism)

- **Constitutional basis:** UUD 1945 and its relationship to Pancasila

### Unit 3: Pancasila in Practice (Weeks 11–14)

- **Pancasila democracy** vs. Western liberal democracy

- Role of Pancasila in law, education, and governance

- Pancasila and human rights in the Indonesian context

- National unity in diversity (Bhinneka Tunggal Ika)

- Pancasila in the modern era: challenges and relevance

- **Case studies:** national development, social harmony, religious tolerance

### Unit 4: Ethics and Professional Life (Weeks 15–16)

- Pancasila values in professional ethics (including science)

- Responsibility of scientists and engineers to society

- **Science for the nation:** How physics contributes to Indonesian development

- Etika profesi: integrity, honesty, service

- Final reflection and examination

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:
1. Explain the historical origins and philosophical content of Pancasila
2. Analyze each of the five Silas and their practical implications
3. Apply Pancasila values to everyday civic and professional life
4. Discuss the role of Pancasila in maintaining national unity and democracy
5. Understand the ethical responsibilities of scientists within the national framework
6. Integrate Pancasila principles into their professional development as physicists
