## 📖 Textbooks — Classical Mechanics

### Halliday, Resnick & Walker — "Fundamentals of Physics"
- **Level:** Introductory (Semester 1-2)
- **Topics:** Newton's laws, energy, momentum, rotation, waves, thermo
- **Chapters:** 1-44 (full coverage of fundamentals)
- **Notes:** Gold standard for first-year physics. Excellent problems.
- **Link:** OpenStax adaptation available free

### Goldstein — "Classical Mechanics"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate/graduate (Semester 3-4)
- **Topics:** Lagrangian mechanics, Hamiltonian mechanics, central forces, rigid bodies
- **Chapters:** 1-10
- **Notes:** THE classical mechanics text. Essential for orbital mechanics.
- **Geodesy link:** Ch 3 (central forces) → satellite orbits

### Marion & Thornton — "Classical Dynamics of Particles and Systems"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate
- **Topics:** Newtonian mechanics, oscillations, gravitation, central forces
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** More accessible than Goldstein. Good problem sets.

## 📖 Textbooks — Electromagnetism

### Griffiths — "Introduction to Electrodynamics"
- **Level:** Intermediate (Semester 3)
- **Topics:** Electrostatics, magnetostatics, Maxwell's equations, EM waves
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** THE standard EM text. Clear explanations, great problems.
- **Geodesy link:** Ch 8-9 (EM waves) → GNSS signal propagation

### Jackson — "Classical Electrodynamics"
- **Level:** Graduate (Semester 3-4)
- **Topics:** Advanced EM theory, radiation, waveguides, special relativity
- **Chapters:** 1-15
- **Notes:** THE advanced EM text. Challenging but comprehensive.
- **Geodesy link:** Ch 11-12 (waveguides) → ionospheric propagation

### Purcell — "Electricity and Magnetism"
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Topics:** EM theory with modern perspective ( Berkley Physics Course)
- **Chapters:** 1-10
- **Notes:** Elegant approach using relativity. Great for building intuition.

## 📖 Textbooks — Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics

### Callen — "Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics"
- **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
- **Topics:** Laws of thermodynamics, thermodynamic potentials, Maxwell relations
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** Rigorous axiomatic approach. Excellent for potential theory.

### Reif — "Fundamentals of Statistical and Thermal Physics"
- **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
- **Topics:** Statistical mechanics, ensembles, quantum statistics, applications
- **Chapters:** 1-14
- **Notes:** Comprehensive. Good bridge between thermo and quantum.

### Pathria — "Statistical Mechanics"
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Topics:** Advanced statistical mechanics, phase transitions, critical phenomena
- **Chapters:** 1-16
- **Notes:** THE graduate stat mech text. Detailed and thorough.

## 📖 Textbooks — Quantum Mechanics

### Shankar — "Principles of Quantum Mechanics"
- **Level:** Intermediate-Advanced
- **Topics:** Hilbert space, Schrödinger equation, angular momentum, perturbation theory
- **Chapters:** 1-20
- **Notes:** Clear, modern approach. Excellent for building foundations.

### Sakurai — "Modern Quantum Mechanics"
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Topics:** Stern-Gerlach, formalism, angular momentum, scattering
- **Chapters:** 1-8
- **Notes:** THE graduate QM text. Physics-first approach.

### Griffiths — "Introduction to Quantum Mechanics"
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Topics:** Wave functions, Schrödinger equation, formalism, applications
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** Accessible first course. Great problems and examples.

## 📖 Textbooks — Relativity

### Hartle — "Gravity: An Introduction to Einstein's General Relativity"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate
- **Topics:** Special relativity, curved spacetime, Einstein equations, black holes
- **Chapters:** 1-26
- **Notes:** Modern, physics-first approach to GR.

### Schutz — "A First Course in General Relativity"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate
- **Topics:** Tensor calculus, Einstein equations, Schwarzschild solution, cosmology
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** Accessible introduction to GR.

### Weinberg — "Gravitation and Cosmology"
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Topics:** Advanced GR, cosmology, gravitational waves
- **Chapters:** 1-16
- **Notes:** Comprehensive graduate text.

## 📖 Textbooks — Fluid Mechanics

### Kundu & Cohen — "Fluid Mechanics"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate/graduate
- **Topics:** Newtonian fluids, potential flow, boundary layers, turbulence, geophysical flows
- **Chapters:** 1-18
- **Notes:** THE standard fluid mechanics text. Excellent for geophysical applications.

### Vallis — "Atmospheric and Oceanic Fluid Dynamics"
- **Level:** Graduate
- **Topics:** Geophysical fluid dynamics, large-scale circulation, waves
- **Chapters:** 1-15
- **Notes:** Essential for geophysical fluid dynamics.

## 📖 Textbooks — Optics & Photonics

### Hecht — "Optics"
- **Level:** Intermediate
- **Topics:** Geometric optics, wave optics, interference, diffraction, polarization
- **Chapters:** 1-14
- **Notes:** Standard optics text. Clear explanations and examples.

### Saleh & Teich — "Fundamentals of Photonics"
- **Level:** Advanced
- **Topics:** Ray optics, wave optics, Fourier optics, laser physics, fiber optics
- **Chapters:** 1-10
- **Notes:** Modern photonics text. Essential for LiDAR and remote sensing.

## 📖 Textbooks — Geodesy Physics

### Heiskanen & Moritz — "Physical Geodesy"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate/graduate
- **Topics:** Potential theory, spherical harmonics, geoid, height systems
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** THE physical geodesy text. Fundamental for geodesy students.

### Torge & Müller — "Geodesy"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate
- **Topics:** Geodetic reference systems, gravity field, height systems
- **Chapters:** 1-10
- **Notes:** Modern geodesy text. Excellent reference.

### Hofmann-Wellenhof — "Satellite Geodesy"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate/graduate
- **Topics:** GNSS theory, positioning, atmospheric effects
- **Chapters:** 1-12
- **Notes:** Comprehensive satellite geodesy text.

### Hofmann-Wellenhof et al. — "GNSS: Global Navigation Satellite Systems"
- **Level:** Advanced undergraduate
- **Topics:** GPS/GNSS positioning, error sources, atmospheric effects
- **Chapters:** 1-10
- **Notes:** Practical GNSS text. Essential for positioning.
