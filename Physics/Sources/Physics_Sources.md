# 📚 Physics Sources — Recommended Textbooks & Online Resources

Comprehensive reference list for all UGM Physics courses, organized by subject area.

---

## 📖 Classical Mechanics

### Primary Textbooks

- **Halliday, D., Resnick, R., & Walker, J.** (2013). *Fundamentals of Physics*, 10th ed. Wiley. — *The standard introductory text for all first-year physics.*

- **Serway, R.A. & Jewett, J.W.** (2018). *Physics for Scientists and Engineers*, 10th ed. Cengage.

- **Tipler, P.A. & Mosca, G.** (2007). *Physics for Scientists and Engineers*, 6th ed. W.H. Freeman.

### Advanced / Analytical

- **Goldstein, H., Poole, C., & Safko, J.** (2001). *Classical Mechanics*, 3rd ed. Addison-Wesley.

- **Landau, L.D. & Lifshitz, E.M.** (1976). *Mechanics*, 3rd ed. Butterworth-Heinemann.

- **Marion, J.B. & Thornton, S.T.** (1995). *Classical Dynamics of Particles and Systems*, 4th ed. Brooks/Cole.

- **Taylor, J.R.** (2005). *Classical Mechanics*. University Science Books.

- **Kleppner, D. & Kolenkow, R.J.** (2013). *An Introduction to Mechanics*, 2nd ed. Cambridge.

---

## 📖 Electromagnetism

- **Griffiths, D.J.** (2017). *Introduction to Electrodynamics*, 4th ed. Cambridge.

- **Purcell, E.M. & Morin, D.J.** (2013). *Electricity and Magnetism*, 3rd ed. Cambridge.

- **Jackson, J.D.** (1998). *Classical Electrodynamics*, 3rd ed. Wiley. *(Graduate level)*

- **Heald, M.A. & Marion, J.B.** (1995). *Classical Electromagnetic Radiation*, 3rd ed. Dover.

- **Zangwill, A.** (2013). *Modern Electrodynamics*. Cambridge.

---

## 📖 Quantum Mechanics

- **Griffiths, D.J.** (2018). *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics*, 3rd ed. Cambridge.

- **Shankar, R.** (2012). *Principles of Quantum Mechanics*, 2nd ed. Springer.

- **Liboff, R.** (2003). *Introductory Quantum Mechanics*, 4th ed. Addison-Wesley.

- **Landau, L.D. & Lifshitz, E.M.** (1977). *Quantum Mechanics: Non-Relativistic Theory*, 3rd ed. Butterworth-Heinemann.

- **Feynman, R.P.** (1965). *The Feynman Lectures on Physics, Vol. III*. Addison-Wesley.

---

## 📖 Thermodynamics & Statistical Physics

- **Kittel, C. & Kroemer, H.** (1980). *Thermal Physics*, 2nd ed. W.H. Freeman.

- **Zemansky, M.W. & Dittman, R.H.** (1997). *Heat and Thermodynamics*, 8th ed. McGraw-Hill.

- **Callen, H.B.** (1985). *Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics*, 2nd ed. Wiley.

- **Pathria, R.K. & Beale, P.D.** (2011). *Statistical Mechanics*, 3rd ed. Elsevier.

- **Reif, F.** (2009). *Fundamentals of Statistical and Thermal Physics*. Waveland Press.

---

## 📖 Optics

- **Hecht, E.** (2016). *Optics*, 5th ed. Pearson.

- **Pedrotti, F.L., Pedrotti, L.M., & Pedrotti, L.S.** (2016). *Introduction to Optics*, 3rd ed. Cambridge.

- **Born, M. & Wolf, E.** (1999). *Principles of Optics*, 7th ed. Cambridge. *(The classic)*

- **Saleh, B.E.A. & Teich, M.C.** (2019). *Fundamentals of Photonics*, 3rd ed. Wiley.

## 📖 Fluid Mechanics & Atmospheric Physics

- **Kundu, P.K., Cohen, I.M., & Dowling, D.R.** (2015). *Fluid Mechanics*, 6th ed. Academic Press.

- **Vallis, G.K.** (2017). *Atmospheric and Oceanic Fluid Dynamics*, 2nd ed. Cambridge.

- **Salby, M.L.** (2012). *Fundamentals of Atmospheric Physics*. Academic Press.

- **Wallace, J.M. & Hobbs, P.V.** (2006). *Atmospheric Science*, 2nd ed. Elsevier.
