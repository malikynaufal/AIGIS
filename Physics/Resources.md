## 1. Core Textbooks

### Classical Mechanics

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Geodesy Relevance |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|
| *Classical Mechanics* | Goldstein, Poole, Safko | 2002 | Orbital mechanics, reference frames |
| *Engineering Mechanics: Statics & Dynamics* | Meriam & Kraige | 2017 | Surveying mechanics |
| *Classical Mechanics* | Taylor | 2005 | Numerical methods for orbits |

### Electromagnetism

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Geodesy Relevance |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|
| *Introduction to Electrodynamics* | Griffiths | 2017 | GNSS signal propagation |
| *Engineering Electromagnetics* | Hayt & Buck | 2012 | Antenna theory, wave propagation |
| *Electromagnetic Waves and Antennas* | Sophocles | 2004 | Satellite communications |

### Quantum Mechanics

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Geodesy Relevance |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|
| *Introduction to Quantum Mechanics* | Griffiths & Schroeter | 2018 | Atomic clocks, precision measurement |
| *Principles of Quantum Mechanics* | Shankar | 2010 | Gravimetry |

### Thermodynamics & Statistical Mechanics

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Geodesy Relevance |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|
| *Thermodynamics & Kinetic Theory* | Rapport | 1970 | Atmospheric modeling |
| *Statistical Mechanics* | Pathria | 2017 | Atmospheric physics |

### Relativity

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Geodesy Relevance |
|------|-----------|------|-------------------|

| *Gravity* | Hartle | 2021 | Gravitational theory |

### Fluid Mechanics
