# Kinetic Theory

> Boltzmann equation, transport coefficients, H-theorem, transport phenomena

> **Part of:** [[Physics MOC]] · [[Physics_Curriculum_Guide]] · [[Study Plan]]

---

## 📚 Core Concept

> **Core idea in one sentence:** Kinetic theory describes the statistical behavior of many-particle systems by tracking their phase-space distribution function.

> **Geodesy Connection:** Collisional transport in ionosphere, neutral atmosphere modeling, and error propagation in measurements.

---

## 🧮 Key Equations

\begin{equation}
\\frac{\\partial f}{\\partial t} + \\mathbf{v} \\cdot \\nabla f + \\frac{\\mathbf{F}}{m} \\cdot \\nabla_v f = \\left(\\frac{\\partial f}{\\partial t}\\right)_{\\text{coll}}
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Boltzmann equation)}

\begin{equation}

\\end{equation}
