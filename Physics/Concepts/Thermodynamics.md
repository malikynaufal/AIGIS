# Thermodynamics

## 📚 Core Concept

> **Core idea in one sentence:** Thermodynamics describes energy transfer between heat and work, governed by universal conservation laws and entropy.

> **Geodesy Connection:** Tropospheric delay modeling; hydrographic surveying; ocean-atmosphere interactions affecting satellite positioning.

---

## 🧮 Key Equations

$$

\begin{equation}
dU = \delta Q - \delta W
\end{equation}
\text{(First law of thermodynamics)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
\Delta S \geq 0
\end{equation}
\text{(Second law — entropy always increases)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
\eta_{\text{Carnot}} = 1 - \frac{T_C}{T_H}
\end{equation}
\text{(Carnot efficiency)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
F = U - TS, \quad G = H - TS
\end{equation}
\text{(Helmholtz & Gibbs free energies)}

$$

### Maxwell Relations

| Relation | Equation |
|----------|----------|
| 1 | $ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial V}\right)_S = -\left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial S}\right)_V$ |
| 2 | $ \left(\frac{\partial T}{\partial P}\right)_S = \left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial S}\right)_P$ |
| 3 | $ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial V}\right)_T = \left(\frac{\partial P}{\partial T}\right)_V$ |
| 4 | $ \left(\frac{\partial S}{\partial P}\right)_T = -\left(\frac{\partial V}{\partial T}\right)_P$ |
