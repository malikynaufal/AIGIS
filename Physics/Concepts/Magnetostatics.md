# Magnetostatics

> Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, magnetic vector potential, magnetic materials

> **Part of:** [[Physics MOC]] · [[Physics_Curriculum_Guide]] · [[Study Plan]]

---

## 📚 Core Concept

> **Core idea in one sentence:** Magnetostatics describes static magnetic fields generated by steady electric currents.

> **Geodesy Connection:** Magnetic surveys for geophysical exploration; instruments like magnetometers in geodesy; Lorentz force in GNSS receivers.

---

## 🧮 Key Equations

$$

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{B} = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int \frac{I d\boldsymbol{\ell} \times \hat{\mathbf{r}}}{r^2}
\end{equation}
\text{(Biot-Savart law)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
\nabla \times \mathbf{B} = \mu_0 \mathbf{J}
\end{equation}
\text{(Ampère's law, differential form)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
\oint_C \mathbf{B} \cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell} = \mu_0 I_{\text{enc}}
\end{equation}
\text{(Ampère's law, integral form)}

$$

$$

\begin{equation}
\mathbf{B} = \nabla \times \mathbf{A}
\end{equation}
\text{(Magnetic vector potential)}

$$
