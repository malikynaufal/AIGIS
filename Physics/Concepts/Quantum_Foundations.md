# Quantum Foundations

> Wave-particle duality, Schrödinger equation, operators, uncertainty principle

> **Part of:** [[Physics MOC]] · [[Physics_Curriculum_Guide]] · [[Study Plan]]

## 📚 Core Concept

> **Core idea in one sentence:** Quantum mechanics describes nature at the smallest scales, where particles exhibit wave-like behavior and measurements are inherently probabilistic.

> **Geodesy Connection:** Atomic clock physics for GNSS satellites; quantum gravimeters; fundamental limits on measurement precision.

---

## 🧮 Key Equations

\begin{equation}
i\\hbar \\frac{\\partial \\Psi}{\\partial t} = \\hat{H} \\Psi
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Time-dependent Schrödinger equation)}

\begin{equation}
\\hat{x} \\Psi = x\\Psi, \quad \\hat{p} \\Psi = -i\\hbar \\frac{\\partial \\Psi}{\\partial x}
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Position and momentum operators)}

\begin{equation}
\\Delta x \\Delta p \\geq \\frac{\\hbar}{2}
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Heisenberg uncertainty principle)}

\begin{equation}
\\langle x \\rangle = \\int \\Psi^* x \\Psi \\, dx
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Expectation value)}
