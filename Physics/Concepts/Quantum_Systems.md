# Quantum Systems

> Harmonic oscillator, hydrogen atom, angular momentum, spin, perturbation theory

> **Part of:** [[Physics MOC]] · [[Physics_Curriculum_Guide]] · [[Study Plan]]

## 📚 Core Concept

> **Core idea in one sentence:** Quantum mechanics provides exact solutions for key physical systems and approximation methods for more complex ones.

> **Geodesy Connection:** Atomic clock physics — hydrogen masers and cesium standards depend on quantum energy level transitions.

## 🧮 Key Equations

\begin{equation}
E_n = \\hbar\\omega\\left(n + \\frac{1}{2}\\right)
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Quantized harmonic oscillator energy)}

\begin{equation}
E_n = -\\frac{m_e e^4}{32\\pi^2\\varepsilon_0^2\\hbar^2}\\frac{1}{n^2} = -\\frac{13.6\\text{ eV}}{n^2}
\\end{equation}
\\text{(Hydrogen atom energy levels)}

\begin{equation}
E^{(1)}_n = \\langle n^{(0)} | H' | n^{(0)} \\rangle
\\end{equation}
\\text{(First-order perturbation theory)}
