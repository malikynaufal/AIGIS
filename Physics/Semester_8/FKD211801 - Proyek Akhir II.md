# FKD211801 — Proyek Akhir II

## Course Overview

The second and final phase of the capstone project — completing data collection/computation, analysis, thesis writing, and public defense. This is the culmination of the UGM physics degree, demonstrating mastery of the discipline through an original contribution to knowledge.

**Contact Hours:** 4 SKS (thesis advising + independent research work)
**Prerequisites:** Proyek Akhir I (completed proposal and preliminary work)
**Co-requisites:** None

---

## 📋 Topics & Outline

### Phase 1: Data Collection and Analysis (Weeks 1–6)

- **Completing measurements** or running full simulations

- **Data processing pipelines:**
  - Raw data → calibration → quality control → analysis
  - Python/NumPy/SciPy data analysis workflow
  - Statistical analysis of results with uncertainty

- **Result interpretation:**
  - What do the results mean in context of the literature?
  - How do results compare with previous work?
  - What are the limitations and assumptions?

- **Problem solving:** adapting methodology when things go wrong

- **Lab/Computational notebooks:** maintaining a detailed logbook

- **Mid-project progress meeting** with supervisor

### Phase 2: Thesis Writing (Weeks 5–12)

- **Thesis structure:**
  ```
  1.  Cover page and title
  2.  Abstract (Indonesian and English)
  3.  Acknowledgments
  4.  Table of Contents
  5.  Chapter 1: Introduction (20-30 pages)
      - Background, motivation, objectives, thesis outline
  6.  Chapter 2: Literature Review (20-30 pages)
      - Comprehensive review of relevant work
  7.  Chapter 3: Methodology (15-20 pages)
      - Detailed description of experiments/computations
  8.  Chapter 4: Results (15-20 pages)
      - Figures, tables, data presentation
  9.  Chapter 5: Discussion (15-20 pages)
      - Interpretation, comparison, implications
  10. Chapter 6: Conclusions (5-10 pages)
      - Summary, contributions, future work
  11. References (minimum 40 peer-reviewed sources)
  12. Appendices: supplementary data, code, derivations
  ```

- **Writing techniques:** scientific English, clarity, structure

- **Figure design:** publication-quality plots, consistent formatting

- **LaTeX thesis template** (UGM format)

- **Citation management** with Zotero/BibTeX

### Phase 3: Review, Defense, and Completion (Weeks 13–16)

- **Supervisor review rounds:** incorporating feedback

- **Internal review:** peer review with fellow students

- **Thesis submission** to department

- **Pre-seminar** (seminar ilmiah): presenting results to physics department

- **Public defense** (seminar sidang):
  ```
  - Presentation: 20-30 minutes
  - Questions from examiners: 15-30 minutes
  - Examiner committee: 2-3 faculty members
  - Grading: presentation (40%), thesis quality (40%), Q&A (20%)
  ```

- **Post-defense revisions:** addressing examiner comments

- **Final submission** of bound thesis to library

- **Congratulations:** degree conferred upon completion!

---

## 🔬 Thesis Writing Checklist

```
□ Title and abstract reviewed
□ All chapters written and reviewed
□ All figures are high-resolution and properly labeled
□ All equations are numbered and referenced in text
□ All references are cited in the bibliography
□ Spell-checked and grammar-checked
□ Formatted according to UGM guidelines
□ Supervisor approval obtained
□ Examiner comments incorporated
□ Final version submitted to library
```

---

## 🎯 Learning Outcomes

Upon completion, students will be able to:
1. Complete an original research project in physics from conception to defense
2. Write a clear, well-structured thesis meeting academic standards
3. Defend their research findings in a public presentation
4. Handle critical questions from examiners professionally
5. Critically evaluate their own work and identify future directions
6. Demonstrate readiness for graduate studies or professional careers
