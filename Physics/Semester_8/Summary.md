## 📈 Learning Progression

### Year 1: Foundations

- **Physics:** Newtonian mechanics → Electricity & Magnetism

- **Mathematics:** Calculus I & II → Differential Equations → Linear Algebra

- **Programming:** Python basics → scientific computing

- **Context:** The central role of physics in understanding Earth

### Year 2: Classical & Modern Physics

- **Physics:** Analytical mechanics → Maxwell's theory → Thermodynamics & Optics

- **Numerical methods:** Solving real problems computationally

- **Laboratories:** Hands-on experiments (mechanics, EM)

- **Foundations:** Quantum & Statistical mechanics introduction

### Year 3: Advanced & Specialized

- **Nuclear, particle, atmospheric, material physics**

- **Relativity & field theories**

- **Signal processing, instrumental techniques**

- **Data management skills for research**

### Year 4: Capstone & Professional

- **Interdisciplinary and frontier physics**

- **Simulation and HPC methods**

- **Capstone research project (2 semesters)**

- **Entrepreneurship and professional ethics**

- **Specialized topics aligned with career goals**

## 🔗 Knowledge Map

```
Mathematics (Calculus, Linear Algebra, Diff Eq)
 ├── Classical Mechanics (Newton → Lagrange → Hamilton)
 ├── Electromagnetism → EM Waves → Optics
 ├── Thermodynamics → Statistical Physics
 ├── Quantum Mechanics → Atomic → Band Theory
 └── Relativity → Field Theory

Physics + Computation → Simulation, Signal Processing, Parallel Computing
Physics + Applications → Geodesy, Atmosphere, Materials, Instrumentation
Physics + Society → Ethics, Entrepreneurship, Sustainability
```

## 🏆 Key Milestones

- **End of Y1:** Competency in Newtonian mechanics, calculus, basic programming

- **End of Y2:** Maxwell's equations, analytical mechanics, lab skills

- **End of Y3:** Quantum mechanics, relativity, computational methods

- **End of Y4:** Research thesis, defense, readiness for career or graduate school
