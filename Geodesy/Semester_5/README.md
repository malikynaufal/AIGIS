# 📘 Semester 5 — Advanced Geodesy: Gravity, GNSS, Cadastre & Modeling

**SKS:** 22 (Compulsory) 
**Focus:** The physics of the Earth (gravity, geoid) and high-precision satellite positioning, integrated with digital terrain modeling and cadastral surveying.

## 📚 Course List & Descriptions

| Code | Course | SKS | Focus |
|------|--------|-----|-------|
| TKD213501 | **Geodesi Fisis** (Physical Geodesy) | 3 | Gravity field, potential theory, Stokes formula, geoid determination |
| TKD213502 | **Model Terrain Digital** (Digital Terrain Model) | 3 | DEM/DTM, LiDAR, SfM, interpolation methods, accuracy assessment |
| TKD213503 | **Administrasi Pertanahan** (Land Administration) | 3 | Land registration, UUPA, PTSL, agrarian legal framework |
| TKD213504 | **Pemrograman Spasial** (Spatial Programming) | 3 | Python/GDAL, automation of geodetic computation |
| TKD213505 | **Survei GNSS** (GNSS Surveying) | 3 | RTK, PPP, carrier-phase, network adjustment |
| TKD213506 | Praktikum Survei GNSS (GNSS Survey Lab) | 1 | Field practice with GNSS receivers |
| TKD213507 | **Survei Hidrografi I** (Hydrographic Survey I) | 3 | Echo sounding, bathymetry, tidal reduction |
| TKD213508 | Praktikum Survei Hidrografi I (Lab) | 1 | Hydrographic field techniques |
| TKD213509 | **Survei Kadastral** (Cadastral Survey) | 3 | Boundary determination, land parcels, PTSL |
| TKD213510 | Praktikum Survei Kadastral (Lab) | 1 | Field cadastral measurement |
| TKD213511 | **Kemah Kerja** (Field Camp) | 1 | Intensive field practice & teamwork |

## 🧭 Learning Outcomes

By the end of this semester, you should be able to:
1. Process GNSS data for base station and rover positioning
2. Compute gravity anomalies and interpret geoid models
3. Generate and analyze Digital Terrain Models
4. Understand Indonesian land administration and registration
5. Execute hydrographic survey procedures
6. Design and implement simple spatial Python scripts
