## 1. Overview

Land administration (*administrasi pertanahan*) encompasses the systems, processes, and institutions through which land rights are identified, recorded, and managed. In Indonesia, this is governed by the **Undang-Undang Pokok Agraria (UUPA No. 5/1960)** and administered by **Kementerian Agraria dan Tata Ruang / Badan Pertanahan Nasional (ATR/BPN)**.

### 1.1 Core Components

The **World Bank** identifies five components of land administration:
1. **Land tenure** (*kepemilikan tanah*)
2. **Land value** (*nilai tanah*)
3. **Land use** (*pemanfaatan tanah*)
4. **Land development** (*pengembangan tanah*)
5. **Land registration** (*pencatatan tanah*)

## 2. Land Tenure (*Kepemilikan Tanah*)

### 2.1 Types of Land Rights in Indonesia (UUPA 1960)

| Right | Code | Description | Max Duration |
|-------|------|-------------|--------------|
| **Hak Milik** (Right of Ownership) | HM | Most complete right; for Indonesian citizens | Permanent (hereditary) |
| **Hak Guna Usaha** (Right to Cultivate) | HGU | Agricultural/bio-productive use | 25–35 years (renewable) |
| **Hak Guna Bangunan** (Right to Build) | HGB | Building on others' land | 20–30 years (renewable) |
| **Hak Pakai** (Right to Use) | HP | Use of state or other's land | 25 years (renewable) |
| **Hak Sewa** (Lease Right) | HS | Rental from the state | 25 years |
| **Hak Membuka Tanah** | HMT | Opening new land for cultivation | As specified |
| **Hak Memungut Hasil Hutan** | HMHH | Collecting forest products | As specified |

### 2.2 Land Ownership Restrictions

- **Foreigners** cannot hold Hak Milik in Indonesia

- Foreigners may hold HGB through *Penanaman Modal Asing (PMA)*

- **WNI (Indonesian citizens)** are primary holders of Hak Milik

- **Adat law** (*hukum adat*) recognizes communal ownership in some regions

## 3. Land Registration (*Pencatatan Tanah*)

### 3.1 Registration Systems

| System | Basis | Scope | Guarantee |
|--------|-------|-------|-----------|
| **Sertipikasi** | Government land | Individual parcels | Full legal certainty |
| **Pendaftaran Tanah** | Customary land | All land | Administrative record |
| **Desentralisasi** | Decentralized | Regional | Regional authority |

### 3.2 Title Certificate (*Sertipikat Hak*)

Two types under **PP 24/1997**:

- **Sertipikat Hak Milik (SHM):** Full title certificate, highest level

- **Sertipikat Hak Guna Usaha (SHGU):** Cultivation right certificate

- **Sertipikat Hak Guna Bangunan (SHGB):** Building right certificate

- **Sertipikat Hak Pakai (SHP):** Right of use certificate

### 3.3 Registration Process

```
1. Permohonan Pendaftaran (Application for Registration)
   ↓
2. Pemeriksaan Berkas (Document Review)
   ↓
3. Pemeriksaan Tanah (Land Examination)
   - Survei Pengukuran (Boundary Survey)
   - Pemeriksaan Hukum (Legal Examination)
   ↓
4. Penetapan Batas (Boundary Determination)
   ↓
5. Pembuatan Peta Survei (Map Production)
   ↓
6. Penyelesaian Peralihan (Resolution of Disputes)
   ↓
7. Penerbitan Sertipikat (Certificate Issuance)
```

### 3.4 Registration Costs

- **BPHTB (Bea Perolehan Hak atas Tanah dan Bangunan):** 5% of transaction value (with threshold)

- **Pajak Penjualan Tanah dan Bangunan (PBB):** Annual tax, varies by location

- **Biaya Sertipikasi:** Government-subsidized for mass registration programs

## 4. Cadastral Survey (*Survei Kadastral*)

### 4.1 Definition

A cadastral survey is the process of identifying and mapping land boundaries for legal and administrative purposes. It produces the **Peta Bidang Tanah (PBT)** — the parcel map that forms the basis of the title certificate.

### 4.2 Cadastral Survey Components

| Component | Description | Reference |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Measurement** | Position determination | UTM/Local grid |
| **Boundary** | Legal limit identification | Based on documents/custom |
| **Area** | Parcel size calculation | Trapezoidal/Simpson's |
| **Documentation** | Legal evidence compilation | PP 24/1997 |

### 4.3 Area Calculation Methods

- **Koordinat (Trapezoidal Rule):**
$$A = \frac{1}{2} \left| \sum_{i=1}^{n} (x_i y_{i+1} - x_{i+1} y_i) \right|$$

- **Measured Distances:** Using traverse or intersection methods

### 4.4 Position Determination Methods

| Method | Equipment | Accuracy | Application |
|--------|-----------|----------|-------------|
| Trigonometri | Total station | ±2–5 cm | Urban parcels |
| Traversing | Total station/GNSS | ±2–10 cm | General cadastral |
| GNSS | RTK/RTN | ±2–5 cm | Large parcels |
| Intersection | Total station | ±3–5 cm | Difficult terrain |

## 5. PPAT (*Pejabat Pembuat Akta Tanah*)

### 5.1 Definition

PPAT is the official who has the authority to make land deeds based on **PP 37/1998** (as amended by **PP 18/2021**).

### 5.2 Authorities

| Authority | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Akta Jual Beli (AJB)** | Sale and purchase deed |
| **Akta Tukar Menukar** | Exchange deed |
| **Akta Hibah** | Grant deed |
| **Akta Pemasukan ke dalam HGU/HGB** | Entry into HGU/HGB |
| **Akta Pemisahan/Penggabungan** | Partition/merger deed |
| **Akta Pembebanan Hak Tanggungan** | Mortgage deed |
| **Akta Pelepasan Hak** | Release deed |

### 5.3 Qualification Requirements

- Indonesian citizen (WNI)

- Minimum education: SMA/SMK (for AJB, diploma/S-1 for others)

- Age: 25 years minimum

- Passed PPAT examination

- Appointed by Kepala Kantor Pertanahan

### 5.4 Geographic Scope

PPAT operates within one administrative district (*kabupaten/kota*) or one sub-district (*kecamatan*).

### 6.1 Organizational Hierarchy

```
Kementerian Agraria dan Tata Ruang / BPN (National)
  ↓
Kantor Pertanahan Provinsi (Provincial)
  ↓
Kantor Pertanahan Kabupaten/Kota (District/City)
  ↓
```

| **Larasita** | Online land registration system | 2014–present |
| **Agraria** | Land redistribution | Ongoing |
| **Percepatan Pendaftaran Tanah** | Accelerated registration | 2013–present |

## 7. Land Disputes and Resolution

### 7.1 Types of Land Disputes

1. **Boundary disputes** (*sengketa batas*) — overlapping claims
2. **Ownership disputes** (*sengketa kepemilikan*) — competing titles
3. **Adat vs. state law** — customary rights vs. certificate
4. **Occupational disputes** (*sengketa penggunaan*)

### 7.2 Dispute Resolution Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Basis | Scope |
|-----------|-------|-------|
| **Musyawarah** (Deliberation) | UUPA Art. 22 | Community level |
| **Pengadilan Negeri** | UU No. 5/1960 | All disputes |
| **Pengadilan Tata Usaha Negara** | UU No. 9/2004 | Administrative disputes |
| **Majelis Arbitrase** | Perjanjian pihak | Contractual disputes |

## 9. Land Redistribution and Reform

### 9.1 Agrarian Reform (*Reforma Agraria*)

Under **PP 57/2016**, the targets include:

1. **Legal reform** (*reforma hukum*): Land registration and legalization
2. **Institutional reform** (*reforma kelembagaan*): Decentralized service delivery
3. **Asset reform** (*reforma aset*): Redistribution of land to the landless

### 9.2 Land Redistribution Targets

- **Social forestry:** 12.7 million hectares

- **Agrarian reform:** 4.4 million hectares

- **Target beneficiaries:** Smallholder farmers, indigenous communities (*masyarakat adat*)

## 10. International Standards

### 10.1 LAS (*Land Administration Standard*)

The **Land Administration Domain Model (LADM)** — ISO 19152 — provides a conceptual framework:

| Package | Component |
|---------|-----------|
| **LA_Party** | Persons and organizations |
| **LA_Role** | Roles of parties |
| **LA_RRR** | Rights, restrictions, responsibilities |
| **LA_BAUnit** | Basic administrative units |
| **LA_SpatialUnit** | Spatial units (parcels) |
| **LA_SpatialSource** | Survey sources |

### 10.2 Voluntary Guidelines on Responsible Governance of Tenure (VGGT)

FAO/World Bank guidelines promoting:

- Legal recognition of land rights

- Gender equality in land ownership

- Indigenous peoples' rights

- Environmental sustainability

## 11. Challenges in Indonesian Land Administration

1. **Incomplete registration:** Only ~60% of land parcels are formally registered
2. **Overlapping claims:** Historical adat rights vs. certificates
3. **Disputes in border areas:** Conflict zones complicate surveying
4. **Digital literacy:** Rural communities may lack access to digital systems
5. **Data integration:** ATR/BPN data vs. BPS (statistical office) data
6. **Corruption:** History of land mafia and fraudulent certificates
