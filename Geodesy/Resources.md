# 📚 Geodesy Resources

> *"Comprehensive bibliography and learning resources for geodetic sciences."*

---

## 1. Core Textbooks

### Physical Geodesy

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Notes |
|------|-----------|------|-------|
| *Physical Geodesy* | Heiskanen & Moritz | 1967 | Classic reference, rigorous |
| *Gravity, Geodesy and Geomagnetism* | Torge & Müller | 2012 | Modern, comprehensive |
| *Fundamentals of Geodesy* | Vanicek & Krakiwsky | 2005 | Rigorous mathematical treatment |

### GNSS

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Notes |
|------|-----------|------|-------|
| *GPS: Theory and Practice* | Hofmann-Wellenhof et al. | 2008 | Standard GNSS textbook |
| *Global Positioning System: Signals, Measurements, and Performance* | Misra & Enge | 2006 | Detailed technical coverage |
| *Understanding GPS: Principles and Applications* | Davis et al. | 2003 | Practical approach |

### Surveying

| Book | Author(s) | Year | Notes |
|------|-----------|------|-------|
| *Adjustment Computations* | Ghilani | 2018 | Least squares, practical |
| *Elementary Surveying* | Wolf & Ghilani | 2017 | Comprehensive surveying |
| *Surveying for Construction* | Irvine & David | 2019 | Construction surveying focus |
