## 📋 Suggested Learning Path

**Year 1:** Engineering Surveying + Geometric Geodesy + GNSS Basics

- Start with surveying fundamentals, ellipsoid geometry, basic GNSS

**Year 2:** Physical Geodesy + Satellite Geodesy + Time Systems

- Study gravity field, satellite measurements, relativistic effects

**Year 3:** Advanced Satellite Geodesy + InSAR + Geodynamics

- Deep dive into InSAR, tectonics, mass transport monitoring

**Continuous:** Research the latest papers from the resources above

- Follow arXiv for newest techniques

- Subscribe to IAG newsletters for updates

> *All sources are legal, free, and open-access (CC, public domain, or government-hosted).*
