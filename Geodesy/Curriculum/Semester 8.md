# 🎓 Semester 8: Synthesis & Graduation

## Overview
The culmination of the Geodesy degree—proving research and technical capability.

## ✍️ Final Milestone

### 1. Skripsi (Final Year Thesis)

- **Process**: Data collection, rigorous analysis, and defense before a board of examiners.

- **Example Topics**: *InSAR for monitoring Jakarta subsidence*, *Indonesian Geoid refinement using airborne gravity*, or *WebGIS for disaster mitigation*.

### 2. KKN (Kuliah Kerja Nyata)

- Community service applying mapping skills to rural development or administrative village mapping.
