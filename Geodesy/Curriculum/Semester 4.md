# 📓 Semester 4: Imaging the Earth

## Overview
Using light and sensors to map the Earth from above—from UAVs to high-resolution satellites.

## ✍️ Core Courses

### 1. Fotogrametri Dasar (Basic Photogrammetry)
- **Topics**: Collinearity equations, interior/exterior orientation, aerotriangulasi, and orthophoto generation.
- **Key Focus**: Converting 2D images into 3D models.

### 2. Penginderaan Jauh (Remote Sensing)
- **Topics**: Multispectral imaging, NDVI, image classification (Supervised/Unsupervised), and atmospheric correction.
- **Data Sources**: Landsat 8/9, Sentinel-2.

### 3. Proyeksi Peta (Map Projections)
- **Topics**: Cylindrical, Conical, and Azimuthal projections. Conformal vs. Equal-area.
- **Indonesia Focus**: [[UTM]] and TM-3 degree zones.
