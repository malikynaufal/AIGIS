# 🏮 Semester 5: The Gravity Field & Satellite Positioning

## Overview
Deep dive into the physics of the Earth's shape and the technology behind centimeter-level GPS positioning.

## ✍️ Core Courses

### 1. Geodesi Fisis (Physical Geodesy)

- **Topics**: Potential theory, Laplace/Poisson equations, Stokes formula, and Geoid determination.

- **Key Formula**: $\Delta g = -rac{\partial T}{\partial r} - rac{2}{r}T$

### 2. Survei GNSS (GNSS Surveying)

- **Topics**: Carrier phase vs. Code pseudorange, RTK, PPP, and tropospheric delay modeling.

- **Practical**: Baseline processing and quality control (DOP, RMS).

### 3. Model Terrain Digital (Digital Terrain Modeling)

- **Methods**: IDW, Kriging, TIN (Triangulated Irregular Network).

- **Source**: LiDAR and SfM (Structure from Motion).
