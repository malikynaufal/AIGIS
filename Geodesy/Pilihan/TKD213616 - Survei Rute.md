# Pilihan: Survei Rute (Route Surveying)

**Kode:** TKD213616 | **SKS:** 3 (2-1) | **Semester:** 6–7

## Course Overview

Route surveying is the branch of engineering surveying concerned with the design, layout, and construction of linear projects such as highways, railways, pipelines, and transmission lines.

## Key Topics

### 1. Horizontal Alignment
- **Simple Curve:** $T = R\tan(\Delta/2) $, $ L = R\Delta\frac{\pi}{180}$
- **Compound/Reverse Curves:** Multiple simple curves
- **Spiral Transition:** Linear change in curvature (Clothoid)

 $ $ l = \frac{R \cdot s}{c}, \quad \text{spiral angle } \phi = \frac{l^2}{2RL}$$

### 2. Vertical Alignment
- **Parabolic curves:** $ y = ax^2 + bx + c $
- **Grades:** $ g_1, g_2 $, rate of change $ r = \frac{g_2 - g_1}{L}$

### 3. Surveying Techniques
- Preliminary surveys (topographic)
- Staking out (setting out points)
- Final as-built surveys
- GNSS-RTK for initial routing

## Study Problems
1. Calculate the tangent length of a curve with radius $R = 500 $ m and deflection $\Delta = 45°$.
2. Explain the use of clothoid spirals.
