# Pilihan: Penataan Agraria dan Tata Ruang (Spatial Planning)

**Kode:** TKD213622 | **SKS:** 3 (3-0) | **Semester:** 6–7

## Course Overview

Spatial planning concepts (RTRW/RDTR), agrarian law implementation in Indonesia, land-use conflict management, and the role of GIS in sustainable territorial development.

## Key Topics

### 1. Planning Instruments
- **RTRW:** Rencana Tata Ruang Wilayah (Province/Kabupaten)
- **RDTR:** Rencana Detail Tata Ruang (Sub-district)
- **Land Use Change:** Regulatory conversion permits

### 2. Agrarian Law (UU No. 5/1960)
- Hak Milik, HGB, HP, HPL
- Agrarian reform principles

### 3. Spatial Tools
- Land Suitability Analysis (Multi-Criteria Evaluation - MCE)
- [[GIS]] suitability models: $Suitability = \sum (Weight \times Factor)$
