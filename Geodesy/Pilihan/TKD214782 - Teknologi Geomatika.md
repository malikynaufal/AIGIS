# Pilihan: Teknologi Geomatika (Geomatics Technology)

**Kode:** TKD214782 | **SKS:** 3 (2-1) | **Semester:** 7–8

## Course Overview

Emerging technologies in geomatics: UAV photogrammetry, mobile mapping (MMS), wearable sensors, IoT for geospatial data collection, cloud-based processing, and artificial intelligence in spatial data management.

## Key Topics

### 1. UAV (Drone) Photogrammetry

| Component | Specification | Application |
|-----------|--------------|-------------|
| Flight controller | GPS+IMU | Autonomous flight |
| Camera | 12–100 MP | Aerial imagery |
| PPP-RTK receiver | 2–5 cm | Direct georeferencing |
